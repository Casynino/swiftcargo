Staff Accounts for the New System

Please prepare the names and email addresses of the people for each role

Swift Cargo is moving all its work — receiving in China, shipping, Dar warehouse, invoices, payments and customer service — into one new system.

Every staff member will have their own personal login, so the system records who did what. To create these accounts we only need each person's full name and work email address. Passwords are not needed on this form — each person will set their own when they first sign in.

How work moves: China Warehouse receives & loads → Dar Warehouse checks in → Accountant prices & invoices → Customer Support informs the customer → Accountant confirms payment → Dar Warehouse releases the cargo. Manager and Boss supervise every step.

1. Boss / Administrator

Full control

Owner of the system with the final say on everything.

  • Sets company details, VAT, storage charges and invoice terms
  • Manages the bank, mobile money and cash accounts printed on invoices
  • Creates staff accounts, sets roles and salaries, removes people who leave
  • Reviews the audit log — every action, who did it and when

2. Manager

Supervision & approvals

Supervises the whole operation and approves decisions.

  • Follows the daily command centre: cargo in China, at sea and in Dar, money owed, alerts
  • Approves credit, pricing, claims and the monthly payroll
  • Reconciles payments and costs against bank and cash records
  • Reviews what each container earned against what it cost; runs monthly reports

3. Accountant (Finance)

All money

Responsible for pricing, invoices, payments, costs and the books.

  • Confirms prices when cargo arrives in Dar and issues invoices (TZS / USD)
  • Records and verifies customer payments; follows up unpaid bills
  • Issues pickup notes for paid cargo; manages credit
  • Records every expense with receipt; counts the office cash; prepares payroll

4. China Warehouse Manager

Guangzhou

Receives goods in Guangzhou, measures them and loads containers.

  • Records each consignment: customer, shipping mark, packages, weight, CBM, photos
  • Prints labels and delivery notes
  • Loads cargo into containers, records vessel and seal number, marks departure
  • Reports damaged or unidentified goods

5. Dar Warehouse Manager

Dar es Salaam

Receives containers in Dar and hands cargo to the right person.

  • Records container arrival and checks every consignment against China's figures
  • Reports missing or damaged cargo with photos
  • Keeps warehouse locations up to date
  • Releases cargo only by scanning the customer's pickup note, only to the receiver

6. Customer Support

Customer service

The customer's point of contact.

  • Answers customer questions and tracks their cargo, container and arrival date
  • Registers customers and keeps their details correct
  • Handles quote, pickup and booking requests from the website and China sourcing
  • Reminds customers about payments and informs them about any cargo issue
